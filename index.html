Getting around (car vs. trains)

Land in Zurich → Lucerne

Direct trains every ~30 min, about 1 hour, no change. If renting a car, pick it up at Zurich Airport; it's ~50 min to Lucerne (A14/A4).

Lucerne ↔ Weggis / Vitznau

The lakeside resorts have no train station. Reach them by the scenic boat across Lake Lucerne (~40-60 min, gorgeous), bus 6/7, or car (~30-40 min). Most lakeside hotels here have free parking.

Day trip to Grindelwald

By car ~1h30 each way; by train via Interlaken Ost ~2h10 each way (the scenic Lucerne-Interlaken Express). Doable as a full day — a car makes it far easier from Weggis/Vitznau.

Onward to Chur (Bernina Express)

Lucerne → Chur ≈ 2h by train (via Arth-Goldau / Zurich). From Weggis or Vitznau, take the boat or bus to Lucerne or Arth-Goldau first, then on to Chur.

Tickets & passes

A Tell-Pass covers boats, buses, trains and most mountain railways (Rigi, Pilatus) in the Lucerne region — worth it if you're not driving. Use the SBB app for point-to-point train tickets.

Car notes

Switzerland requires a motorway vignette (usually included on rentals). Lakeside villages are small; hotels with garages / free parking are flagged on each card.
